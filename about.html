Hi! I’m Tianzi, a graduate student in Computer and Information Science at the University of Pennsylvania.

I’m interested in building robots that can learn to move, interact, and adapt. My current interests center around robot learning, particularly reinforcement learning for humanoid locomotion and manipulation. Before coming to Penn, I worked at Sentigent Technology on learning-based locomotion and motion skills for wheeled-legged robots, which sparked my interest in how learned policies can translate from simulation to real robots.

I received my B.S. in Computer Science from Vanderbilt University, where I also explored LLM-based multi-agent systems.

Outside of robotics, I love photography, wandering around new cities, and spending time with my cat!
